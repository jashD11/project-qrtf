# Phase 5 — selection gate and regime layer: falsification results, and the direction left

**Status: two of three proposed ideas are falsified. This document records why, and redirects
the phase at the one lever the tests did not touch.**

Phase 4c closed as a negative result — all six frozen cells fail the DSR gate, best 0.017
against a 0.95 threshold ([`phase4c_results.md`](phase4c_results.md)). Phase 5 was proposed
as a revival along two linked lines:

1. a second selection stage conditioning the traded deciles on per-name trend;
2. a refined regime detector whose narrow job is to modulate the **short leg's** exposure
   rather than gate the whole book.

The brief asked for the cheap kill-tests to run *before* anything was built, and for the
work to stop and report if the alpha did not survive. It did not. §2 and §3 are the negative
results; §4 is what remains.

Every number below is regenerated by `scripts/phase5_falsification.py` from committed
artefacts — `python scripts/phase5_falsification.py`, ~2 min, read-only. Nothing is
hand-entered.

## 1 · Three corrections to the framing, before the results

### 1.1 The gate does not contain the regime model

`RegimeResult.states`, `.probs` and `.stress` have **zero consumers anywhere in the repo**.
The only field read is `.panic` (`run_pipeline_ml.py:349`), and `panic` never touches the
HMM (`src/production_ml/tier2_regime.py:421-424`):

```python
stress_full = z[[FEATURE_COLUMNS[VOL_IDX], "avg_corr"]].mean(axis=1)
panic_full  = self._severity_gate(stress_full)      # > expanding 85th percentile
```

The live gate is the mean of two causal z-scores — log realised vol and average pairwise
correlation — thresholded at a trailing quantile. The fitted Gaussian HMM is decorative.

This is the real reason `hmm_states` is frozen at 2 (`config.py:397`, and the note in
CLAUDE.md that a 2-state and 3-state run are "return-identical"). It is not a modelling
subtlety. Nothing downstream reads the states.

**Consequence for any estimator work:** signed features, a jump model, a different state
count — all are no-ops as currently wired. An estimator upgrade must ship with the Tier 3
rewiring that makes the model's output reachable, or it changes nothing.

### 1.2 The gate fails its own sanity check

The proposed tripwire: correlate the gate against a 21-day realised-vol z-score; above ~0.80
it is a rolling vol estimate wearing eight parameters. Measured on the repo's own Phase 4
index data (`data/bhavcopy/index_daily.parquet`, 2,810 days, 2014-02-03 → 2025-06-30):

> **corr(`z_log_realized_vol`, 21-day realised-vol z) = 0.907**

and that is the dominant term of `stress`. The diagnosis was right but understated: the gate
is not a vol classifier that emerged from fitting returns, it is a vol z-score by direct
construction. It is not wearing the eight parameters.

**Signed features do fix this.** Feasibility probe on 19 years of NIFTY (`^NSEI`),
**in-sample, not a backtest**:

| observation layer | fwd-21d return, hi vs lo state | realised vol, hi vs lo | corr w/ vol-z |
|---|---|---|---|
| raw return + vol | +0.24% vs +1.22% | 20.4% vs 12.9% | 0.731 |
| signed EWM (mean / log downside-dev / Sortino, halflives 5/10/21) | **+1.29% vs +0.09%** | 14.6% vs 19.3% | −0.503 |

Raw returns split variance. Signed features split direction. The mechanism is real and
reproduces on our data.

### 1.3 Filtered inference is already correct in production — but not in SANDBOX

`src/production_ml/tier2_regime.py` decodes by a hand-rolled forward recursion
(`_forward_filter`, line 221): `log_alpha` propagated with `_logsumexp`, no backward pass.
Baum-Welch appears only in `_fit_block`'s `model.fit` (line 330). There is no
`predict_proba`, `score_samples`, `decode` or `predict` in the file. Refits are walk-forward,
504-day trailing train → 63-day disjoint forward block (lines 366-434).

**Flagged as requested:** `src/sandbox_run/tier2_regime.py` *does* fit on full history —
`model.fit(obs)` on all observations (line 54) then `model.predict(obs)`, which is Viterbi
global MAP (line 55). Phase 1 SANDBOX only; it never touches the PRODUCTION_ML path. But any
regime number quoted from Phase 1 is not out-of-sample and should not be cited as such.

### 1.4 The history ceiling is 2007, not 1990

NSE's `ind_close_all` archive 404s before 2013-01-02 (`bhavcopy_download.py:59-64`; probed
and documented at [`phase4_plan.md`](phase4_plan.md) §124-131). Measured alternatives:

| source | bars | from | quality (measured) |
|---|---|---|---|
| yfinance `^NSEI` | 4,660 | 2007-09-17 | 0 NaN closes, 4 stale closes, 0 duplicate dates, 240-250 sessions/yr |
| yfinance `^BSESN` | 7,195 | 1997-07-01 | a different index — different constituents and free-float rules |
| local NSE archive | 3,082 | 2013-01-01 | the current floor |

The premise that **episode count binds, not day count**, is correct. 2007 buys 2008 GFC,
2011 EU, 2013 taper, 2015-16 China, 2018 IL&FS, 2020 COVID, 2022 rates — roughly 8 episodes
against today's 3.

**And the cross-section is what pins the floor, exactly as suspected.** Of the three
observation inputs, `mkt_ret` and `log_realized_vol` already come from a real published
NIFTY-50 close series (`_load_index_daily`, line 119) — not a synthetic composite. Only
`avg_corr` needs the stock panel, and the panel starts 2013. Signed features are index-only,
so **swapping the observation layer removes the constraint that caps history.** The two
proposed changes are one change.

## 2 · The trend overlay is falsified

Run on cached walk-forward output — no refit, no new data.
Source: `data/trial_database/wf_cache/ac11679c42e4feee` (`daily_nse500`,
`tgt_fwd_logret_5b`), prices `data/nse500_daily_ohlcv.parquet`. 2,337 days × 1,030 names,
2016-01-21 → 2025-06-27.

### 2.1 Orthogonality: passes

Cross-sectional Spearman ρ(alpha score, trend), averaged over dates:

| trend measure | full cross-section | **within top decile** | within bottom decile |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12-1 momentum | 0.321 | **−0.011** | 0.089 |
| 63-day | 0.058 | −0.018 | 0.033 |
| 21-day | −0.124 | −0.008 | −0.004 |
| price vs 200dma | 0.167 | −0.038 | 0.081 |
| 63-day Sortino | 0.053 | −0.034 | 0.037 |

The structural argument holds. The ranker trains on cross-sectionally demeaned features and
is genuinely blind to absolute trend: inside the traded decile the correlation is ≈0. A
post-ranking trend condition is new information, not a recycled feature.

**But orthogonal is not the same as useful.**

### 2.2 Does it pay? No — on every variant, before any cost

Weight matrices built directly and multiplied by forward returns. Gross, so the comparison
does not depend on the cost model:

| variant | L/S ann | **SR** | max DD | book beta | worst month |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline equal-weight | 68.3% | **4.97** | **−10.9%** | −0.05 | **−6.1%** |
| smooth tilt, strength 0.5 | 64.2% | 4.42 | −12.9% | −0.02 | −7.7% |
| smooth tilt, strength 1.0 | 66.1% | 4.01 | −19.0% | −0.00 | −12.6% (2020-06) |
| hard cut, top/bottom half | 66.5% | 3.45 | −24.5% | +0.02 | −17.1% (2020-06) |
| reversed (contrarian) tilt | 58.7% | 4.36 | −9.1% | −0.08 | −4.4% |

**Every variant is worse than doing nothing** — on return, Sharpe, drawdown and worst month
simultaneously. Sharpe runs 4.97 → 4.42 → 4.01 → 3.45 monotonically as the tilt sharpens.
The optimal tilt strength is zero.

**Continuous tilt does dominate the hard cut**, as predicted: SR 4.01 vs 3.45, max DD −19.0%
vs −24.5%, on a book that falls from 49 to 22 names a side. The breadth argument
(IR ≈ IC·√BR) is confirmed. It is simply not enough to make either version pay.

**The worst month is the predicted momentum crash.** 2020-06, the rebound off the COVID low:
baseline −6.1%, tilted −12.6%, hard cut −17.1%. The overlay loads directly into the turn.

**But it is not a beta story, and this contradicts the proposed mechanism.** The expectation
was that fallen names carry inflated beta, leaving a dollar-neutral book meaningfully short
beta into a rebound. Measured against NIFTY-50, book beta is ≈0 for every variant (−0.05 to
+0.02). The damage is idiosyncratic and volatility-concentration, not short-beta. Which means
beta-neutral sizing and the attribution regression against a plain momentum spread — the two
proposed controls — target something that is not happening.

It is also not a cost story: turnover **falls** under the tilt (long leg 0.860 → 0.762/bar,
−11%). It fails gross, on risk-adjusted return, before a single basis point.

Five trend families were tested (12-1, 63d, 21d, price-vs-200dma, 63d Sortino), at two tilt
strengths, plus the hard cut and the reversed sign. All point the same way.

### 2.3 The one open caveat

The tilt was applied to raw decile masks, without the `rebalance_buffer_mult=2.0` hysteresis
the production path applies first (`tier3_execution.py:231`). The margin is wide and the
buffer reduces churn — which the tilt already does — so this is unlikely to reverse the
ranking. It is unconfirmed, and closing it is step 1 of §5.

## 3 · Short-leg modulation: the ceiling is low

The gate's job was narrowed to modulating short-leg exposure. Two measurements bound what
that can be worth.

### 3.1 The feasible short leg is nearly worthless gross

Applying the point-in-time borrowable set (`data/bhavcopy/shortable_mask.parquet`, mean 182
eligible names/day) and re-ranking inside it the way `run_execution` does — filter the
*scores*, then fill toward `k` (B2):

| short leg | standalone ann | standalone SR |
|---|---|---|
| unrestricted | 35.9% | 1.51 |
| **SLB-feasible** | **5.0%** | **0.22** |

86% of the short leg's standalone return lives in names that were never borrowable —
consistent with Phase 4c's gross SR 4.20 → 1.88 on the same restriction.

### 3.2 And running it costs about what it earns

From the repo's own recorded run
(`data/trial_database/phase4c_dsr_matrix_execution_diagnostics.csv`, 21b target):

| cell | turnover/bar | trade drag p.a. | borrow drag | gross SR | net SR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `long_only` | 0.242 | 15.96% | — | 1.120 | **+0.297** |
| `long_short_slb` | 0.338 | 20.60% | 0.82% | 0.958 | **−0.567** |

Adding the feasible short leg costs **+5.46%/yr** (4.64% incremental trade drag + 0.82%
borrow) to buy **~5.0%/yr** of gross alpha. The leg's entire edge and its own cost cancel.

A gate can only scale exposure in [0, 1]. It would be modulating something with no expected
value to modulate. This does not say the gate is bad — §1.2 says it is — it says the target
was chosen wrong.

### 3.3 The ceiling test that was started and not finished

The correct kill-test is an **oracle gate**: perfect foresight of the short leg's next-day
return, leg on only when it wins. If an oracle cannot clear DSR 0.95, no real gate can.

A scratch reconstruction produced `long_only` at net SR **−0.76** against the recorded
**+0.297**, because it omits the rebalance buffer and so runs turnover at 0.86/bar instead
of 0.242. The cost path was wrong, so **no number from that run is quoted anywhere in this
document.** The test is sound and has to run inside `run_execution`. It is step 2 of §5.

## 4 · The direction left: cost, not signal

Neither falsification touched the following, and it is where Phase 4c's own "not ruled out"
list already pointed.

`long_only` / `tgt_fwd_logret_21b` is the only cell in the frozen grid with positive net
Sharpe (**+0.297**). Its **gross** Sharpe is **1.120** and its trade drag is **15.96%/yr**.
The signal is not the binding constraint; cost is. Phase 4c reached the same conclusion from
the other side — the 21-day target cut turnover 0.45 → 0.24 and was "the one lever with
headroom."

So the live hypothesis for Phase 5 is: **attack turnover, not signal.** Candidates, in the
order their cost per unit of evidence suggests:

1. **Cost-aware portfolio construction.** The book currently re-ranks and refills toward `k`
   with a symmetric two-band buffer. A construction that prices the *spread it is about to
   pay* into the entry decision — rather than charging it afterwards — is the direct attack
   on a 15.96%/yr drag. All the panels needed already exist
   (`spread_daily_cs.parquet`, `adv_daily.parquet`, `sigma_daily.parquet`).
2. **Beta and sector neutralisation.** Phase 4c listed this as out of scope, needing its own
   frozen prior and its own N. Note §2.2's finding that book beta is already ≈0 on the
   dollar-neutral book — so the case for this is weaker than it looked, and it should be
   measured before it is built.
3. **The regime layer on its own terms.** §1.1 and §1.2 establish a real structural defect
   independent of what it gates. Rebuilt with signed features on 2007-onward history and
   judged on **downside metrics** — max drawdown, worst single rebound month, vol — against
   NIFTY buy-and-hold and against the current gate, it is a defensible contribution even
   with the cross-sectional alpha dead. It is a risk-reduction result, not a return result,
   and should be reported as one.

## 5 · Plan

Steps 1 and 2 are kill-tests. Step 3 does not begin before both report.

### Step 1 — close the §2.3 caveat in-engine

Add a trend-tilt option to `build_weight_matrix` behind a `ML_CONFIG` flag defaulting
**off** (the repo's opt-in discipline). Re-run the §2.2 table through `run_execution` so the
rebalance buffer, participation cap and real cost panels apply. Expected: ranking unchanged,
tilt still loses Sharpe. Write to the **sensitivity ledger**, never the headline one.

### Step 2 — oracle ceiling test for short-leg gating

Add a short-leg exposure scalar to `run_execution` (a `pd.Series` in [0, 1], default 1.0).
Run always-on, always-off and oracle on the `long_short_slb`/21b cell. Report net Sharpe and
DSR at N=30. **If the oracle does not clear 0.95, short-leg gating is dead** and §5 step 3
does not run as specified — redirect to §4 item 1.

### Step 3 — regime rebuild, judged on downside metrics

Conditional on step 2, and scoped as §4 item 3 (a risk result, not a return result):

- **Observation layer** — replace the 3 raw inputs with signed EWM features (mean, log
  downside deviation, Sortino) at halflives 5/10/21, reusing `_causal_zscore`
  (`tier2_regime.py:197`). Index-only, which unlocks the longer history.
- **History** — new loader emitting the 7-column schema `_load_index_daily` expects;
  `bhavcopy_panel.build_index` (line 759) documents that contract, so integration is a path
  swap in `_RegimeConfig`. Reconciled against the local archive on the 2013-2025 overlap.
- **Estimator** — keep the HMM as baseline; add `jumpmodels` (0.1.1 on PyPI, sklearn API) as
  a comparator behind a flag.
- **Wire it in** — `build_weight_matrix`'s `dynamic_tilt` branch already builds
  `flag = panic_bool.to_numpy(float)[:, None]` (`tier3_execution.py:214-223`), a float column
  vector. Swapping it for a continuous [0, 1] short-leg score needs no change elsewhere. Six
  call sites pass `panic`.

**λ selection.** Shu/Yu/Mulvey (2024) do cross-validate the jump penalty on strategy
performance. That is not transferable here: [`phase3_deferred_hmm.md`](phase3_deferred_hmm.md)
§4 requires structure knobs be chosen by held-out likelihood *inside* the walk-forward, never
by realised returns, and tuning on PnL is precisely the selection bias the DSR gate corrects.
The paper spans 1990-2023 across three markets; we would have one market and ~8 episodes.
Select by held-out likelihood; every λ in the grid still enters the trial ledger.

### Step 4 — index-series data quality

The stock panel is already held to a high standard — ISIN-keyed, detected corporate actions,
six acceptance gates in `bhavcopy_validate.py`, three blocking. A spot-audit found it healthy
(0 NaN closes, 0 zero-volume name-days, no phantom sessions; 388 symbols map to >1 ISIN,
which is the rename handling working as designed). **Nothing in the repo validates the index
series.** Add a *generated* `data/reports/index_quality.md` — every figure computed, none
hand-entered — covering calendar, stale prints, phantom sessions and splice reconciliation on
the overlap, exiting non-zero on a dirty series.

### Step 5 — trial ledger

Every threshold, penalty, halflife and state count is its own trial family, including ones
tried and discarded. `config.TRIAL_LEDGER` (line 278) already charges 1 cell for a rejected
panic-gate variant with the rationale "tested and rejected — still a search"; the same
accounting applies to everything in §5.

**One plumbing trap.** `panic_threshold` and the other `_RegimeConfig` fields are **not** in
`StrategyConfig`'s hash (`config.py:440-498`). Any regime knob promoted to a real axis must
enter that hash, or ledger cells silently overwrite each other. The sensitivity path works
around this today by string-prefixing (`run_pipeline_ml.py:475`).
